PROJECT 2  ·  AEROFOIL WIND TUNNEL LABORATORY

 

Experimental aerodynamics laboratory testing an aerofoil section across a range of angles of attack in a closed‑circuit wind tunnel. Lift, drag, and pitching moment were measured using a multi-axis force balance and compared against XFOIL theoretical predictions.

Equipment & Methodology

  closed‑circuit wind tunnel with Aerotech smoke generator for flow visualisation

  Hot-wire anemometer confirming freestream velocity of 11.68 m/s throughout test

  TQ Education multi-axis force balance resolving lift, drag, and pitching moment to ±0.01 N

  Aerofoil model tested at incremental angles of attack (α); data compared against XFOIL

 

Aerofoil model in wind tunnel - α = 0°

Smoke visualisation of flow over aerofoil

Instrumentation & Force Balance Readings

Measurements at two angles of attack show increasing lift and pitching moment with α. Key finding: lift coefficient increased linearly up to the stall angle, consistent with thin aerofoil theory. Drag polar agreed with XFOIL predictions below 8°.

Design Process

PROJECT 4  ·  AUTOMOTIVE DIAGNOSTICS - GARAGE WORK

 

Hands-on automotive diagnostic and repair work carried out independently. Each case follows a structured Symptom → Diagnosis → Repair workflow, prioritising systematic root-cause analysis over speculative part replacement.

 

Case 1 - Anti-Roll Bar Drop Link Replacement

Symptom

Diagnosis

Repair

Excessive play detected in tyre when tested vertically during vehicle inspection.

Physical inspection confirmed worn drop link with excessive ball joint articulation.

The drop link connects the anti-roll bar to the suspension, reducing body roll in corners.

Removed failed drop link using impact driver and socket set.

Fitted new OEM‑spec drop link, torqued to specification. Play eliminated on retest.

 

Drop link - worn ball joint

OEM replacement fitted

 

Case 2 - Cylinder 4 Misfire (OBD Codes: P0304 / P0300)

Symptom

Diagnosis

Repair

Rough running reported. OBD scan revealed P0304 (Cyl 4 Misfire) and P0300 (Multi-Cylinder Misfire) - both current faults on ECM.

Component-swap method applied:

1. Swapped coil cyl 4↔1 - fault stayed on cyl 4 → coil ruled out.

2. Swapped spark plug cyl 4↔1 - fault stayed on cyl 4 → plug ruled out.

3. Compression test → intake manifold gasket leak confirmed.

Fault isolated to intake manifold gasket on cylinder 4.

Systematic diagnostic process over speculative replacement - fault resolved at root cause.
